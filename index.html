Windows desktop creator workflow

Turn long livestreams into publish-ready content.

Veyskap Clip Studio is an AI-assisted desktop application for creators. It analyzes long-form livestream recordings, identifies useful moments, prepares short-form and long-form edits, and lets the creator review and approve content before publishing to connected social platforms.

See how it works Privacy Policy

Creator-controlled workflow

AI helps find and prepare content; the creator stays in control of account connections and publishing decisions.

AI highlight detection

Analyze long recordings and surface candidate highlights, scenes, reactions and moments worth editing.

Clip preparation

Prepare short-form clips, metadata and editing suggestions for review inside the desktop application.

Connected publishing

Creators can connect supported social accounts with OAuth and explicitly approve publishing actions.

Veyskap Clip Studio does not ask for or store social-platform passwords. Account authorization is performed through the platform's official OAuth flow.

Social integrations

Supported integrations may include YouTube, Kick, TikTok and Instagram. Availability and publishing capabilities depend on each platform's API access, permissions and review requirements.
